# Submitting your guide

We welcome community contributions to our official documentation! Whether you've written a quick how-to or a detailed technical walkthrough, your input helps other users learn and solve problems faster.

This guide explains the process for submitting your guide for consideration and inclusion in our documentation platform.

### Prerequisites

Before submitting, please ensure that:

* You've proofread your guide and reviewed it for accuracy, clarity, and completeness.
* You've written the guide in the `Markdown` format and followed our required directory structure. (There are guides available on how to accomplish this using [Google Docs](writing-a-tech-guide-using-google-docs.en.md) or [Ghostwriter](writing-a-tech-guide-using-ghostwriter.en.md)
* You've added credits and a last modified date (or updated them if you've modified an existing guide).
* It is desirable for new guides to follow the style of other guides for a uniform look across the documentation.

### Submission channels

You can submit your guide using one of the following methods:

**Option #1: GitHub Pull Request**

If you're familiar with Git and GitHub, you can use this method.

1) Fork our documentation repository.
2) Add your guide under the appropriate section, following the existing directory structure.
3) Commit your changes with a descriptive message and push.
4) Open a pull request with a brief summary of what your guide is all about.

**Option #2: Discord**

1) Join our [community Discord](contact-us.en.md).
2) Create a ZIP archive of your guide and all of its assets (images, diagrams, ...).
3) Message one of the tech support staff, including a short summary describing the purpose of your guide, and attach the aforementioned archive.

### Review process

Once submitted, our documentation team will review the guide for technical accuracy, and suggest edits if needed. Your guide will be approved and published afterwards.

### Licensing and attribution

By submitting your guide, you agree to:

* Grant us permission to edit and redistribute the content.
* Optionally include your name or handle for attribution (let us know your preference).
